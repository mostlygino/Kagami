Kagami 鏡

See what is on the wire. A Mac packet and flow viewer built on Wireshark's own dissectors.

This installs Kagami __VERSION__ in Applications. You can also link the kagami command into /usr/local/bin; choose that under Customize.

Before you record: Kagami reads and records packets with Wireshark's tools. Install Wireshark if you don't have it (or brew install --cask wireshark), and include its ChmodBPF helper so you can capture without sudo.

Flow files (nfdump, CSV, JSON, Zeek conn.log) open without Wireshark.

Needs

  • macOS 26 or later, Apple silicon
  • About 15 MB of disk space
